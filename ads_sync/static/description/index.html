Meta Integration - Facebook Ads & CAPI Sync

Seamlessly connect Odoo CRM/Sales with Meta Business Suite

The Meta Integration addon bridge the gap between your Odoo system and Facebook's advertising ecosystem. Automate lead generation and conversion tracking to optimize your marketing ROI and sales efficiency.

Key Features

Facebook Lead Webhook

Automatically sync leads from Facebook Lead Forms directly into Odoo CRM in real-time. Never miss a potential customer again.

  • Instant response to potential leads
  • Automatic source tagging as "Facebook"
  • Comprehensive data mapping (Name, Email, Phone)

Meta Conversions API (CAPI)

Send Purchase event data directly from Odoo Sales to Meta Server to improve ad attribution and lower acquisition costs.

  • Accurate ROI measurement
  • Bypass browser cookie limitations
  • Secure data hashing (GDPR compliant)

How It Works

The module establishes a secure handshake between your Odoo instance and Meta's servers.

Facebook Lead Ads → Meta Webhook → Odoo CRM Lead
Odoo Sales Confirmation → Meta CAPI → Facebook Pixel Optimization
